## How it started

We built *To Be Continued.* together on 26–27 November 2026 at the University of Glasgow’s Advanced Research Centre, during the [RSE Learning Environment Hackathon](https://personalhel.github.io/RSE-hackathon/).

We spent much of those two days talking about what gets lost when research code changes hands. Which notebook produced the figure in the paper? Why did we abandon the first approach? Which step does someone still do by hand?

Those conversations shaped the questions in this guide. Pick a question you wish someone had answered for you, jot down what you know, and leave the resulting `HANDOVER.md` alongside the code.

## Working with an agent

Point your agent to the [handover instructions for agents](../llms.txt). Ask it to read through your project, draft the answers it can support, and interview you about the rest. You can open the resulting `HANDOVER.md` here to review or edit it.
